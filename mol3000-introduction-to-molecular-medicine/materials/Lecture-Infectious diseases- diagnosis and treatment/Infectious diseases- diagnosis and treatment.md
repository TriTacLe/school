## Infectious diseases- diagnosis and treatment
Kilde: Canvas, MOL3000, modul "Lecture-Infectious diseases- diagnosis and treatment"

Hi, learning goals for the topic Infectious diseases includes that the student should be able to:

•describe the role of infectious diseases in a global context, and which factors that have led to a decline in mortality of such diseases

•describe the role of normal microbiota in humans

•describe why some microorganisms are pathogenic to humans

•describe the microbial targets of antimicrobial drugs

•describe how antimicrobial resistance (AMR) of develops, the main molecular mechanisms of such resistance, how antimicrobial susceptibility can be tested in the laboratory and how we can combat AMR

•describe microbiological methods that are most used in the diagnosis of infectious diseases

•describe how we can prevent spread of infection

•explain the meaning of terms used in molecular medical microbiology

The lecture will focus mainly on bacterial infections, while chapter 5 and 6 in thye book Molecular Medicine An Introduction by Kurreck and Stein also includes viral, fungal and parasittic diseases. Exam questions will cover both that presented in the lecture and the two book chapters.
