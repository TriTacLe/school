## Syllabus (Canvas, BI1007)
## Velkommen! / Welcome! to **BI1007: Plant structure and function**

***Time: Autum semester 2026***

**Course Description**

During this course you will examine the structure and function of vascular plants, with an emphasis on the relationships between anatomy, physiology, growth, development, and environmental adaptation. Using the life cycle of flowering plants as an integrative framework, you will explore how plants acquire and transport water and nutrients, perform photosynthesis, regulate growth and development, and respond to biotic and abiotic environmental cues.

Topics include plant cell, tissue, and organ structure; primary and secondary growth; photosynthesis; water relations and solute transport; mineral nutrition and toxicity; hormonal regulation of growth and development; tropic responses; flower, fruit, and seed development; and senescence and dormancy. You will also explore anatomical and physiological adaptations that enable plants to thrive in diverse environments.

The three laboratory exercises will provide you with hands-on experience with research approaches in plant physiology, including seed germination and mineral nutrition using living plant material. You will expose tomato plants to different nutritional regimes and assess their growth and development throughout the semester. With the laboratory work, data analysis, and written reports, you will develop practical skills in experimental design, data interpretation, scientific communication, and collaborative problem-solving.

Upon successful completion of the course, you will understand the relationships between plant structure and function from the organ to the whole-plant level, explain the physiological processes underlying plant growth and development, evaluate plant adaptations to different environments, and interpret experimental data using established methods in plant physiology.

**I hope you all enjoy learning about plants!**

### Læringsutbytte / learning outcomes

**Learning outcomes**

After completing the course you should have a good understanding of the relationship between plant structure and function at the organ to organism level. You should have also acquired knowledge of plant adaptations to different environments.

The course has the following learning goals in knowledge, skills, and general competence:

*Knowledge*

You will be able to:

🌱 Diagram and explain the function of plant cell, tissue, and organ types

🌱 Explain the role of central physiological and metabolic processes in plant growth and devlopment

🌱 Compare and contrast anatomical and physiological adaptations to different environments

🌱 Explain classic experiments and methods in plant physiology research

*Skills*

You will be able to:

🌱 Acquire and apply key concepts in plant physiology

🌱 Use relevant professional tools and techniques

🌱 Analyze and interpret physiological measurements and results

🌱 Carry out a task in the lab both independently and with others

🌱 Reflect on your own understanding of plant physiology

*General competence*

You will be able to:

🌱 Collect and analyze data using appropriate tools and software

🌱 Present and discuss scientific results in a synoptic way

🌱 Convey key subject matter in writing

🌱 Work with plants in a laboratory and greenhouses
