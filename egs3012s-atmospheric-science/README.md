## EGS3012S Atmospheric Science
UCT, Department of Environmental and Geographical Science. The atmosphere as part of a changing climate system, read from a southern African perspective: climate variability and forcing, biological responses, quantifying change, then thermodynamics, dynamics and synoptic forecasting. Lectures Mon to Fri 08:00-08:45, EGS Building Studio 5, face to face. Slides go up on Amathuba at the end of each week. Full source: [[materials/EGS3012S 2026 Handbook.pdf|course handbook]].

## Assessment
Final mark: 3 tests 10 percent, project 10 percent, 10 pracs 20 percent, exam 60 percent.
- **Tests**: Fridays 08:00-08:45 in Studio 5, class time, 14 Aug, 25 Sep, 23 Oct. Be seated by 07:55. MCQs, possibly short answer.
- **Pracs**: Tuesday or Wednesday 14:00-17:00 weekly from 28 July, Studio 5. Sign up for a day on Amathuba (max 30 per day). Each prac is 2 percent. At least 5 of the 10 are invigilated in person under the new Science Faculty 80 percent rule.
- **Project**: A0 virtual poster on one African extreme climate or weather event from 2016 to 2026. Individual, and the event must be unique across the class. Due 19 October 2026 17:00. Brief and rubric are in `project/`.
- **DP**: attendance and completion of all pracs, tests and project, plus class record at least 40 percent. A serious attempt at the project is itself a DP requirement.
- **Exam**: in person, 3 hours, date set by the examinations office. Subminimum 40 percent in the exam. A supp needs at least 40 percent course mark and all other subminima met, and a passed supp gives a generic UP, no number.
## Submission rules
Amathuba only, never email. PDF, under 5 MB, named `STUDENTnum_EGS3012S_Prac_n_YYYYMMDD.pdf` with no spaces. Late costs 5 percent per day for 4 days, then 0. Resubmissions get a new timestamp and can be penalised. Everything goes through Turnitin.

Citations: inline author-date or numeric, consistent throughout, every inline cite in the bibliography, DOI or URL on every entry that has one. AI policy and the plagiarism declaration template are in `materials/reference/`.
