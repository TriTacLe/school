## Lab information
Kilde: Canvas, TTK4147, modul "Labs"

There are in total 9 lab exercises numbered 0-8. At least 80 % of them have to be approved in order to be eligible for the final exam.

The labs are held on a weekly basis, you choose **one **of the timeslots; either Thursday 08:15-12:00 or Friday 08:15-12:00 at [Sanntidssalen](https://use.mazemap.com/#v=1&campusid=1&zlevel=2&center=10.401353,63.418584&zoom=19&search=g203&sharepoitype=identifier&sharepoi=325-G203) (G203/G204). 

Labs start in week 35 and end in week 43. Each exercise should be completed and approved in each timeslot i.e. exercise 0 in week 35, exercise 1 in week 36 and so on and so forth till exercise 8 in week 43. 

 

Lab exercises are done in groups of two, join and register for a [group](https://ntnu.instructure.com/courses/24093/groups) before week 35. The groups are divided by timeslot and workstation number at the lab, for example the group "Thursday 3" are at the lab on Thursdays and uses workstation number 3.
