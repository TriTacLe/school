## Text book
Kilde: Canvas, TTK4147, modul "Curriculum"

We mainly use Stallings *Operating Systems: Internals and Design Principles, Global Edition* as the main source of material in this course. The book can be found at [Akademika.](https://www.akademika.no/teknologi/data-og-informasjonsteknologi/operating-systems-internals-and-design-principles-global-edition/9781292214290)

In addition, some parts are covered by Burns & Wellings *Real-time systems and their programming languages*, 4th edition. 

A few other selected readings can also be found under the module "Curriculum".
