Staff and Communication

We are pleased to introduce to you the team members available to teach, guide and assist you through this course.  Typically, you can expect a response from us within a 24-hour period, and during office hours only. The team is here to help you; please do not hesitate to contact us.

Announcements
Staff will make all course announcements using the Amathuba Announcements tool, and key dates will be posted in the Amathuba Calendar. Please check your email frequently to see if any announcements have been made.

Course material questions
Please reach out to your tutors first if you are facing difficulties with the coursework. Should you (individually, or with other classmates) have any general course-related concerns or issues, kindly contact your elected Class Representative(s) first as they are the intermediary between the class and the convener(s).  

Administrative queries
If you have any questions relating to course administration, personal difficulties that are private in nature, or any problems affecting your ability to effectively engage with the course, we encourage you to contact your course convener(s), lecturer(s), or the teaching assistant directly to have your concerns addressed and a solution discussed.

Additional support
We encourage you to form small study groups to support each other while engaging with the course material.
