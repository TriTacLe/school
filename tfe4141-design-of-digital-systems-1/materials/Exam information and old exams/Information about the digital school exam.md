## Information about the digital school exam
Kilde: Canvas, TFE4141, modul "Exam information and old exams"

The exam in this course is a so-called "digital school exam". This means that the exam will be held using Inspera Assessment on a PC in a location with an invigilator ("eksamensvakt"). Depending on the location, you will need to use your own laptop or an NTNU desktop PC (if the exam takes place in a PC lab). I strongly recommend everybody to check out the information about this type of exam on

[https://innsida.ntnu.no/wiki/-/wiki/English/Digital+school+exam+-+for+students](https://innsida.ntnu.no/wiki/-/wiki/English/Digital+school+exam+-+for+students)

Note in particular the information on how to hand in calculations/hand-drawings on paper. This will (most likely) be needed on this exam.

To get to know better what exams in Inspera Assessment look like, I encourage you to check out their [demo page](https://ntnu.inspera.no/#LIST).

Note also the following examination arrangement related to what you are allowed to bring with you on the exam day:

Permitted examination aids [C: Specified printed and hand-written support material is allowed. A specific basic calculator is allowed.](https://i.ntnu.no/wiki/-/wiki/English/Permitted+examination+aids)

In this case the support material is as follows:
• The textbook: Dally et al., “Digital design using VHDL, a systems approach”
• Four handwritten / hand-drawn A4 pages with your own notes / figures (two double sided or four single sided sheets of paper). If you want to use a tablet to make your pages that is ok, but they must be hand written and printed.
