## Lecture schedule
Kilde: Canvas, TFE4141, modul "Curriculum and Schedules"

The purpose of the course is to learn to design digital systems. You will learn this through lectures, assignments and the term project. For each lecture in the lecture schedule below, relevant chapters in the book: "Digital design using VHDL, a systems approach" (See Curriculum) will be listed. Links to slides will be made available for each topic. Note that the lecture schedule is subject to change throughout the semester.
