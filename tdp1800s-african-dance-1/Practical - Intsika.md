Practical stream of [[African Dance 1]]. Tuesdays 17:30-19:00, Dance School, Lower Campus. Worth 50% of the course.
## The technique
- Intsika means "pillar" or "strength" in isiXhosa. Pan-African studio technique created by Maxwell Rani in 2002, drawing core movements from across the continent and the diaspora.
- Philosophy: the thousands of dance cultures in Africa carry inherent techniques. Intsika stylises them for studio teaching, "African dance pedagogy of pluralism", old to new frameworks.
- Covers West, East, Central and Southern African dances plus percussion, rhythm and song workshops. Beginner level.
- Worth holding against the theory: Intsika is exactly the synthesis Mabingo warns about, except it is authored, named and built from inside. See question 1 in [[Discussion prep#Questions to put to the room]].
## Class traditions (graded as part of technique)
- Dobale: Yoruba gesture of respect, opens and closes every class. Teacher first, then students. Approach the drummer with bent knee, eye contact, right hand on heart, then palm down on the ground.
- Counting: shortened Kiswahili numbers. "ja, li, tu, ne" = moja, mbili, tatu, nne (1-4). "no, ta, ba, ne" = tano, sita, saba, nane (5-8). First set is the default count-off.
- End-of-class salutation: collective gesture sequence thanking heart, ground, sky, teacher, drummer and fellow students.
## Dress and etiquette
- Women: lapa (wrap-around skirt, about two yards of fabric) and comfortable tight top. Men: vest and three-quarter loose pants or shorts.
- No shoes, socks, footed tights, loose jewelry. Hair tied back. Phones off.
- Late, injured or sick students attend anyway, assist with percussion and journal the class.
## Movement vocabulary to know
Neo, Doople, Olmec, Zepia, Kouitchin, Dada, Lilly pads, Zulu stance, Gula, Matari, Mpheyane, Pickups, Gijima, Iceba, Butterfly, Tulism, Ibo, Guinea Fowl, Python Head
## Practical exam, 20 October
Three parts.
- Centre warm-up: dynamics, synchronisation, breath, execution, performance style, musicality.
- Across the floor: interpretation, movement quality, precision, articulation, music in movement.
- Combination: overall understanding, ease, individuality, aesthetics, dynamics, energy, presentation.
