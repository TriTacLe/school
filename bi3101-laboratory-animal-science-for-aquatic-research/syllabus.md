## Syllabus (Canvas, BI3101)
### Welcome to the course BI3101 Laboratory Animal Science for Aquatic Animals!

The target group for this course is people who will work with live aquatic animals in research. 

People who are planning to work with rodents, pigs or model fish are encouraged to take the national [CAREiN course.](https://www4.uib.no/en/faculty-of-medicine/department-of-clinical-medicine/studies-at-the-department-of-clinical-medicine/carein-national-online-course-in-laboratory-animal-science)

The course is structured in modules which give you an overview of the course content. Some modules concern general laboratory science, and these are not species-specific.  Other modules are species-specific, that is, tailored towards animal research using aquatic animals.

You can move through the modules by clicking the Previous and Next arrows at the bottom of the pages. From the left-hand menu you can also go directly to different sections and tools in the course.
