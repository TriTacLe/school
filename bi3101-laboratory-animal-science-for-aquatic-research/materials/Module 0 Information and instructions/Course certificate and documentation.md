## Course certificate and documentation
Kilde: Canvas, BI3101, modul "Module 0 Information and instructions"

After completion of the theoretical course, you will get the course credits and a course certificate documenting that you have completed the theoretical part of the required education and training in laboratory animal science. 

The practical training must be documented on a separate practical training certificate, which will be issued from the institution where the practical training took place. 

Together, this will be the documentation of your education and skills in the field of laboratory animal science. You are **strongly** encouraged to keep this documentation. Your animal establishment will want to see this kind of documentation before you can function as a user in the establishment. Additionally, when applying to the national authorities (the Food Safety Authorities in Norway) for permission to do animal research, you will need to document your qualifications.

Later on, documentation of continued training will also be needed (see next page).

 

**Completion of theoretical course only:**

 

 

You will get course credits. 

You will not be qualified to perform animal experiments

**Completion of theoretical course + species-specific practical training:**

 

 

 

 

 

 

You will get course credits from the theoretical course.

You will be qualified to perform procedures that you have received training in, on the correct animal species.

You are expected to continue to maintain and develop your knowledge and skills

Continuous education and training is a requirement to continue to be qualified.
