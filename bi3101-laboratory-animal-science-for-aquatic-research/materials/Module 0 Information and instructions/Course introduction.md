## Course introduction
Kilde: Canvas, BI3101, modul "Module 0 Information and instructions"

This course is developed for persons who will work with aquatic animals for research. If you are not going to work with research animals, the course may not be right for you.

 

The full education and training in laboratory animal science consist of both theoretical training and practical training. This course (BI3101) covers the theoretical part of the required education for persons who will perform procedures on animals (EU Function A), plan and design procedures and projects (EU Function B), or kill animals (EU Function D).

The course is directed towards research on aquatic animals. People who will work with rodents, pigs or model fish should instead sign up for the national CAREiN course. More information about CAREiN can be found [here](https://www4.uib.no/en/faculty-of-medicine/department-of-clinical-medicine/studies-at-the-department-of-clinical-medicine/carein-national-online-course-in-laboratory-animal-science). People who will work with wildlife animals or field experiments, should contact the Norwegian University of Life Sciences to get information about courses for field work.
