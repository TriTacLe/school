## Practical training
Kilde: Canvas, BI3101, modul "Module 0 Information and instructions"

The full education and training in the field of laboratory animal science consists of both theoretical training and practical training. 

This course covers only the theoretical training. You will get course credits from NTNU after passing the theoretical training. 

But in order to become qualified to perform procedures with animals, or kill animals, you will also need practical training. 

The practical training is not part of this course. You will need to organize the practical training at your home institution, make sure to have this done under supervision, and have your practical training documented. 

#### **Requirements for the practical training:**

- The practical training must happen with the **same species**, and the **same techniques**, as the candidate will use during their continued work with live animals

- The practical training must happen under supervision

- The supervisor must have up-to-date competence regarding the species and the techniques

- There is no pre-set determination regarding the number of days or hours. The duration of the practical training will depend on how quickly the candidate can learn to perform the techniques at an acceptable level, and how challenging the techniques are.

- The practical training must happen under supervision until the candidate can demonstrate skills sufficient to perform the procedures humanely and to satisfaction, with safe working practices for both animals and operators.

- The practical training must be documented

In many cases, the institutions have templates for the practical training, which must be filled when the training is finished. The training document must be signed by the trainer.
