## After the course
Kilde: Canvas, BI3101, modul "Module 0 Information and instructions"

After completion of the course, a course certificate will be issued as documentation that you have passed the theoretical education. The course certificate will be sent by email to your registered NTNU email address. You will get study credits (ECTS) from NTNU after completion of the course. When you get your practical training, this also needs to be documented because you will need to present such documentation to your laboratory animal facility and the Food Safety Authorities. 

The course certificate, together with potential supplementary documents of the candidate's skills, are documentation of the education and training in laboratory animal science and the candidate's qualifications. You are strongly encouraged to keep the documents and make sure you have a backup, as such documentation is often requested in research establishments in many countries before one can be registered and approved as a user. Additionally, when applying to the competent authorities for permission to do animal experiments, documentation of education and training is needed. In case documents are lost, you may need to complete the entire education and training programme again to be allowed to work with animals in a research animal facility.

After the initial practical training, the candidate will be allowed to work under supervision from the research group until the candidate has demonstrated the requisite competence. The person supervising the candidate's work must have appropriate and up-to-date knowledge as well as competence and experience from the species of animals and relevant  techniques that the candidate will use for his/her future research. 

**Please note**: If you are planning to perform future work with research animals in another country, you may be required to complete national or local course modules or training programmes to comply with national requirements in that country. For example, you probably need to learn about the national legislation in that country, which will be somewhat different from the Norwegian legislation. If working in another country is relevant for you, we recommend that you familiarize yourself with the requirements in that country.

After completing the course and obtaining relevant practical training, the candidates are expected to maintain and develop their competence through a process of Continuing Professional Development (CPD). This is to ensure that everybody who are involved in the use and care of research animals remain up-to-date in their field. The candidates are encouraged to contact their laboratory animal facility to get suggestions for CPD activities.
