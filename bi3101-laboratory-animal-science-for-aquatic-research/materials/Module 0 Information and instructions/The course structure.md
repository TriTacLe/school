## The course structure
Kilde: Canvas, BI3101, modul "Module 0 Information and instructions"

**The theoretical course is organized as one general part and one part for aquatic animals.**
 

-
**The general part:** The majority of this part will be self-studies using digital course material available in Canvas modules. Supplementary lectures will be given through Zoom. See schedule for details.

-
**Everyone will need to study the course modules in the general part and complete the individual online student activities in each module in the general part. **

-
**The individual student activities in the general part are mandatory and a prerequisite for being allowed to complete the course and take the exam. **The deadline for the individual student activities in the general part is October 18th (end of the day). Students who don't complete the individual activities within the deadline are not allowed to complete the course.

-
**The special part for fish/aquatic animals** will take place during weeks 44 and 45. This part will happen as auditorium lectures. **Physical attendance is mandatory** and a prerequisite for being allowed to take the exam.
