## Course examination
Kilde: Canvas, BI3101, modul "Module 0 Information and instructions"

The course has one final written exam towards the end of the semester. All students will need to complete and pass the exam to pass the course.

#### **Prerequisites for taking the exam**

Before taking the exam, all compulsory activities must be completed and passed. This means:

- Completing all the course modules in the general part, including tests and assignments in the modules.

- Participation at the teaching activities in the part for fish and aquatic animals

- Active participation in the group work in the part for fish and aquatic animals, including the evaluation of the group work 

Students who don't complete these mandatory activities, will not be allowed to take the exam.

#### **Written exam**

The written exam will take place on December 21st from 09:00 to 13:00. The written exam will consist of a combination of multiple-choice-questions and questions that need a written answer. There will be one general part and one special part, corresponding to the structure of the course. Both the general part and the special part must be passed in order to pass the exam. More information about the written exam will be sent to you closer to the exam date.
