## Legal background
Kilde: Canvas, BI3101, modul "Module 0 Information and instructions"

Laboratory animal science is defined as a multidiciplinary branch of science that will contribute to the humane use of animals in biomedical research and to the collection of informative, unbiased and reproducible data.

According to national and international legislation, everyone who will use animals for scientific purposes needs to be adequately educated and trained before they can carry out procedures on animals, design procedures and projects, take care of animals, or kill animals. This course will fulfil the demands for the theoretical education for functions A, B and D (see below) and it will focus on fish and aquatic animals for research.

The course in laboratory animal science aims to follow the requirements in the [Norwegian regulation on animal experimentation](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2015-06-18-761), annex E.

#### EU functions:

- A: People who carry out procedures on animals

- B: People who design procedures and projects

- C: People who take care of animals

- D: People who are killing animals
