## Syllabus (Canvas, BI3072)
### Welcome to BI3072 - Environmental Toxicology

The world is in the midst of a [triple planetary crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_planetary_crisis), that is, the joint impact of climate change, biodiversity loss, and pollution, all of them deeply interconnected. Our course will take you deep into the pollution aspect of that crisis. It will provide you with an advanced understanding of toxicology, both in terms of the environment and human health. We will examine the principles of how pollutants affect biological systems across scales and inspecting diverse types of pollution. The course will also challenge you to think about the societal conditions that promote pollution and - in turn - can be changed to prevent it.

Before starting, please take a look at the [course description](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/BI3072#tab=omEmnet) to learn about the course and its requirements.

### Learning outcomes

- understand fundamental goals, approaches and principles of toxicology

- understand how toxicant affect health and environment as well as challenges involved

- apply this understanding to investigate and assess impacts of toxicants on health and the environment

### Preparation/Textbook

The course uses diverse resources but the main content is available in **[Casarett & Doull's Essentials of Toxicology](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/mhp/product/casarett-doull-s-essentials-toxicology-fourth-edition?pd=search&viewOption=instructor#mh-ecomm-he-product-accordion-2010273490-heading-mh-ecomm-accordion-list-ffeee7fbf3) (4th edition)**. It is available in the library and local bookstores. If you want to dig deeper, there is an extended version of the book: [Casarett & Doull’s Toxicology: The Basic Science of Poisons](https://accesspharmacy.mhmedical.com/book.aspx?bookid=2462) (9th edition).

In preparation for each class, we ask students to read the corresponding chapters. Please see the [schedule in this pdf](https://ntnu.instructure.com/courses/26216/files/1098581?location=course_syllabus_26216&wrap=1) to find the chapters to prepare.
