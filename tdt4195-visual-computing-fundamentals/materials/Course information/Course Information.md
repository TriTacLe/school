## Course Information
Kilde: Canvas, TDT4195, modul "Course information"

### This is the first of 3 courses in Visual Computing and is composed of two parts:
- Computer graphics - CG (first half of the course)

- Digital image processing - IP (second half of the course)

Two follow-up courses in Visual Computing can be taken in Spring 2027:

- TDT4230  **Graphics & Visualization**

- TDT4265  **Computer Vision & Deep Learning**

**See also [https://www.idi.ntnu.no/grupper/vis/teaching/#courses](https://www.idi.ntnu.no/grupper/vis/teaching/#courses)**

You can see the graphics **achievements** of previous students (mostly in the follow-up TDT4230 course) here:  [https://www.idi.ntnu.no/grupper/vis/teaching/#student-projects](https://www.idi.ntnu.no/grupper/vis/teaching/#student-projects)

**Evaluation:** The assignments will count for 40% of the final grade, while the final exam will count for 60% of the grade. Assignments and the final exam are graded separately and you need to pass both to get a final grade. The final grade is a weighted average of the assignments and the final written exam. More specifically A-E are converted to numbers 5-1. If for example you get A on the assignments and C on exam then the final grade will be: 5*0.4 + 3*0.6 = 3.8 => B with normal rounding rules applied.

**Course Material**: Course material consists of the lecture slides (uploaded on Canvas), sections from recommended books, additional notes on Canvas and any extra material provided by the Teaching Assistants in the labs. 

The Graphics part of the course is based on the book 'Graphics & Visualization' by T. Theoharis et al. However, this book is currently being updated for a new edition and the relevant updated chapters are being made available via Canvas.

The classical Image Processing part is based on 'Digital Image Processing, Global Edition by Rafael Gonzalez, Richard Woods' fourth edition.
