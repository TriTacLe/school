## Assignment Policy
Kilde: Canvas, TDT4195, modul "Course information"

Late DeliveryThe assignments in this course count towards the final grade.
We therefore use a strict policy in how we will handle late submissions.
This policy counts for *all *assignments.

Specifically:

- If you submit late, but within **12 hours** after the deadline, the maximum score of the assignment **reduced by 1**.
- If you submit late, **between 12 and 18 hours** after the deadline, the maximum score of the assignment is **limited to 3**.
- If you submit late, **between 18 and 24 hours** after the deadline, the maximum score of the assignment is **limited to 2**.
- If you submit **over 24 hours** after the deadline, the maximum score is limited to **0**.PlagiarismFor all assignments in this course, you are either allowed to work on your own, or in pairs.

We do not tolerate plagiarism *(i.e. submitting someone else's work as your own without listing a source)*, such as from the internet or other students taking this course.

We actively look for such copying when correcting assignments.

Use of AIUse of AI is permitted, but we ask that each student use it with respect to their own learning.

Keep in mind that AI may make nefarious mistakes and directly plagiarise with no warning.
