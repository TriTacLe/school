### Course content
The officiel course description is found here:

[Course - Very Large, Distributed Data Volumes - TDT4225 - NTNU](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TDT4225#tab=omEmnet)

The course has three differnt partial evaluations (delvurderinger)

1. Exercise 2, programming with MySQL and BigData. 20%
2. Exercise 3, programming with MongoDB and BigData, 20%
3. Final exam, Sluppen - 3 Dec 15.00 (3pm) - 18.00 (6pm). 60%

Modules give you an overview of the course content. You can move through the modules by clicking the Previous and Next arrows at the bottom of the pages.

From the left-hand menu you can also go directly to different sections and tools in the course.
