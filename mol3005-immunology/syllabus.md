## Welcome!

Welcome to the MOL3005 course in "Immunology".

You find a course description, learning outcomes, lecture rooms ("timeplanen") and exam info [here](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/MOL3005#tab=omEmnet). 

Modules give you an overview of the course content, lecture slides, suggested reading material and colloquia discussion tasks. You can move through the modules by clicking the Previous and Next arrows at the bottom of the pages.

From the left-hand menu you can also go directly to different sections and tools in the course.
