## Syllabus (Canvas, TTT4234)
## Velkommen! / Welcome!
The Space Technology I-course is an introduction to space technology and space-oriented activities. Selected topics are presented: Mechanics in Space. Weightlessness and microgravity research. Rocket technology. Satellites. Observational satellites, environmental observations. Communication and communication systems. Man in Space. Norway in space, Norwegian space industry. 

![](https://ntnu.instructure.com/courses/24126/files/1114125/preview?location=course_syllabus_24126)

In the course, we aim to cover a basic introduction to each of the topics, adding in links to relevant research topics and showing industrial relevance. The course aims to present possibilities, both at NTNU and in industry/international. 
### Teaching methods
**Lectures**. Several given by central national and international experts within their field, from universities, industry, and the government. 

**Exercises**. Non-mandatory but highly recommended. Some will be carried out in joint sessions, some are for self-immersion. (High relevance for the exam...)

**Feedback **through dialogue, digital quizzes and similar.

### Læringsutbytte / learning outcomes
The full course description can be found here: [https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TTT4234/2026/1#tab=omEmnet](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TTT4234/2026/1#tab=omEmnet) 

**After the course, a candidate should be able to: **

- describe the characteristics of the different orbit types (LEO, MEO, GEO, HEO)

- describe at least three main areas of application for satellites (earth observation, navigation, and communication)

- set up a link budget and discuss this

- describe the main components of a satellite (payload, rocket engine, propellant, antennas, solar cells and mechanical structure)

- describe the effects of microgravity and radiation on organic and non-organic materials.

**General competence**: The candidate can understand suitable satellite missions, the pros and cons of operating in the space environment, and the tasks an engineer must accomplish to secure a successful launch and operation of a satellite in orbit.
