**File format
**

Do not deliver assignments as zip files, but instead upload files individually. Uploading zip files makes the grading process more difficult, and might cause your submission to recieve a failed grade.

 

**Groups**

You may submit assignments in groups of 1 or 2. To sign up for a group, first click "Assignment X Group Sign-Up Sheet", and then click the group you want to join. It is not possible to leave a group once you have joined, so please be careful when joining. After joining a group, the group assignment delivery should be visible.

Each group is only used for a single assignment. If you want to do another assignment with the same person, you both need to sign up for a new group associated with the new assignment.

**IMPORTANT:** Both members must be in the group before submitting. Only group members that are in the group at the time of submission will recieve a grade. If you are unsure whether both members were in the group when you submitted, please resubmit.
