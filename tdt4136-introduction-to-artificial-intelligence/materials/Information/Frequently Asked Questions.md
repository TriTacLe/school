**FAQ**

**Assignments**

- Are assignments mandatory?

- Yes, you need to pass all 3 mandatory assignments to be eligible for the exam

- I have a question about an assignment

- Search the forum to see if it has been asked before

- If not, post your question on the forum

- I signed up for the wrong group

- Send an email to Espen ( espen.b.hogstedt@ntnu.no )

- I didn't get a grade for an assignment even though the grading deadline has passed

- Send an email to Espen ( espen.b.hogstedt@ntnu.no )

- I need an extension to the deadline

- Explain why in an email to Espen ( espen.b.hogstedt@ntnu.no )

**Lectures**

- Is lecture attendance mandatory?

- No, lecture attendance is not mandatory. If you prefer not to go to lectures, or to just watch lecture recordings, you are free to do so.

- The "Lecture Recording" page does not work

- This is likely due to some issue related to cookies. To fix the issue, login to Canvas in a private window or in a different browser.

**Lab Hours**

- Are lab hours mandatory?

- No, lab hours are not mandatory. They are simply an additional resource for students.

- Why is there 4 different time slots?

- There are different time slots so that you can pick the time that is most convenient for you :)

**Assignment Lecture****s
**

- Are assignment lectures mandatory?

- No, assignment lectures are not mandatory. They are simply an additional resource for students.

- Will assignment lectures be recorded?

- Yes, assignment lectures will recorded the same way as regular lectures.

**Exam**

-  I have two exams at the same time, what should I do?

- See NTNU's [FAQ page regarding exams](https://i.ntnu.no/wiki/-/wiki/English/Exam+-+Frequently+asked+questions+for+students)

- Are previous exams representative of the current syllabus?

- Mostly, but some topics such as the GraphPlan algorithm are no longer in the current syllabus. The exam will only cover topics within the current syllabus.
