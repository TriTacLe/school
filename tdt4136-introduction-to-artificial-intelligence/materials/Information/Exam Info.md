## Exam Info
Kilde: Canvas, TDT4136, modul "Information"

**General information
**

Information about the time and location of the exam can be found on StudWeb and the ["about page"](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TDT4136/#tab=omEksamen), but we also provide information here for your convenience. In the case of conflicting information, the information on this page should be considered out of date.

 

Exams at NTNU are organized centrally, this means that as course administrators we cannot change the date or location of the exam.

 

Note that there may be multiple exam locations, you need to check StudWeb to find the location assigned to you.

 

Please see NTNU's [help page regarding exams](https://i.ntnu.no/en/eksamen) and NTNU's [FAQ page regarding exams](https://i.ntnu.no/wiki/-/wiki/English/Exam+-+Frequently+asked+questions+for+students) for more general information.

 

**Details**

**Examination arrangement:**   School exam, 4 hours, written. 
**Date: ** 07.12.2026
**Exam language:** Questions only in English, you can answer in English or Norwegian
**Examination aids:**  [D](https://i.ntnu.no/wiki/-/wiki/English/Permitted+examination+aids). None.
**Grade:** Letter grades based 100% on final exam. 3 mandatory assignments must be delivered and passed ("godkjent") in order to take the exam. We will use the [percentage grading scale](https://i.ntnu.no/wiki/-/wiki/English/Grading+scale+using+percentage+points).
