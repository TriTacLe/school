**Assignment****s: **

There will be 3 mandatory assignments, all of which must be delivered and ***passed*** (“**godkjent**”) in order to be able to take the exam. Two of these require programming.

 

Assignments will be given out on Wednesdays at 9 AM and will be delivered on Fridays at 23:59. The dates for the three assignments will be:

- Assignment 1: Search

- Will be given out: 26th August

- Delivery deadline: 11th September

- Assignment 2: Constraint Satisfaction Problem

- Will be given out: 9th September

- Delivery deadline: 25th September

- Assignment 3: Adversarial Search

- Will be given out: 7th October

- Delivery date: 23rd October
