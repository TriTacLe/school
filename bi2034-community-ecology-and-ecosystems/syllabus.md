## Syllabus (Canvas, BI2034)
## Welcome to BI2034 Community Ecology and Ecosystems!

This course consists of a field course part, a project part and a theoretical part. In other words, we want to give you a practical and theoretical introduction to how to study community ecology and ecosystems. We focus on two ecosystem types. Freshwater ecosystems where you take samples from lakes North of Trondheim around the field station in Snåsa, and a coastal heathland ecosystem near our field station Slettvika, West of Trondheim. The Snåsa field station can only accommodate 12 students, whereas Slettvika can accommodate 50 students. Most students will therefore do terrestrial projects and two groups of students will do the freshwater field course. In groups, you will gather your own data, define research questions to be studied and learn to process your data with statistical tools in R. You will write the project up as a scientific paper and present your research questions, methods and results at two presentations, again in groups.

Apart from this, the course consists of of ca 12 double lectures where we teach you the theories in community ecology and a final exam. Your project work counts 40% and the written exam 60% of your grade from the course.

The field course and the project work is mandatory, whereas the lectures are not mandatory (but the exam is based on the lectures).

Because many foreign students normally apply for attending this course the entire course is taught in English. Only in the case no foreigners has signed up for we will switch to Norwegian.

The main syllabus of the course is Begon & Townsend (2021) Ecology - from individuals to ecosystems. We recommend to buy the book from former students or at Akademika in physical format for a better learning experience

You can read more about the course here: [https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/BI2034#tab=omEmnet](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/BI2034#tab=omEmnet)
