Staff and Communication

We are pleased to introduce to you the team members available to teach, guide and assist you through this course.  Typically, you can expect a response from us within a 24-hour period, and during office hours only. The team is here to help you; please do not hesitate to contact us. Please use the course email to contact the staff: eee3094s@uct.ac.za.

Announcements
Staff will make all course announcements using the Amathuba Announcements tool, and key dates will be posted in the Amathuba Calendar. Please check your email frequently to see if any announcements have been made.

Course material questions
Students will have the opportunity to receive help from tutors during tutorial sessions. In periods of high demand, such as before a test or deadline, we will potentially schedule hot seats to make more time with the tutors available. Tutors are not expected to arrange meetings with students or answer questions via DM or email - don't forget, they are also students trying to get their own work done so their capacity to engage outside of the designated contact sessions may be limited.

The lecturers and TAs will be available for consultation by appointment only to handle more intensive questions, mark queries and personal matters.

Course Feedback
Should you (individually, or with other classmates) have any general course-related concerns or issues, kindly contact your elected Class Representative(s) first as they are the intermediary between the class and the convener(s).  

Administrative queries
If you have any questions relating to course administration, personal difficulties that are private in nature, or any problems affecting your ability to effectively engage with the course, we encourage you to contact your course convener, lecturers, or teaching assistants directly to have your concerns addressed and a solution discussed.

Anonymous Inbox
The course convenor will set up an anonymous inbox using Google forms so you can give us feedback or ask questions comfortably. Check the introductory announcements each week to receive the link.
