Course runs as four modules of three weeks each: two teaching weeks then one consolidation and assessment week. Lectures Mon and Thu 10:00-12:00 in AC Jordan LT3A. Tutorials Wed 14:00-16:00 in Snape TS2C.

Note the content area on Amathuba groups notes into three modules with nine chapters, while the schedule table uses four modules. The schedule table governs pacing. Note file numbering (1_1, 5_2, 8_4 and so on) is a legacy flat sequence and does not match chapter numbers.

## Labs
Three labs building to a controller for a "kittycopter":
1. System identification of aircraft suspension, separate from the kittycopter
2. Proportional controller design and system ID of the kittycopter
3. First-order compensator design
Control lab open Mon to Fri 9:00 to 17:00, walk-in help from Mr De Maar. Book 1-hour slots under the Groups tab. Exemptions are all three labs or none, repeating students only.
